The problem

The call comes in while you're showing another house. By the time you call back, they've called the sign on the next street.

Buyers and renters call the moment they see a listing, and they call three agents in a row. Whoever picks up first and can actually answer the question gets the showing. Voicemail loses; a generic answering service that can't say how many bedrooms loses almost as badly.

Evenings and weekendsare when buyers call, and when you're at showings, open houses and closings.
The same ten questionsbeds, baths, price, pets, available when, is it still available, can I see it Saturday.
Paperwork before the showingshowing agreements, the IABS notice, applications, "text me your license number".

What agents told us

  • "I lose rental leads every weekend. By Monday they've signed somewhere else."
  • "I need the showing agreement signed before I unlock a door. Chasing that signature is half my texting."
  • "A call service can't tell a renter whether the house takes dogs. So the renter hangs up."
  • "I manage twelve rentals for a client. The tenants call my cell for everything, including the water heater."
  • "If a seller calls, I need to know that minute, not in a voicemail at nine o'clock."

What Donna does

Your listings, your calendar, your forms. Handled on the first call.

Knows your listings

  • Answers by address, street, nickname, zip, bedrooms or budget: price or rent, beds and baths, square feet, lot, pets, deposit, available date, HOA, year built.
  • Says "four bedrooms plus a study" the way you would, and counts it when someone asks for five.
  • Knows what's active, pending, under contract or leased, and never offers a showing on a home that's gone.
  • Import your listings from your MLS export in one step, or add them by hand. Change a status and she knows on the next call.

Books the showing

  • Offers only the times you've opened, at the length you set, with travel time between showings.
  • Texts the caller a confirmation with the address; texts you the booking with the caller's name and number.
  • Showing agreement goes out for signature automatically; the showing is confirmed once it's signed (or right away, your choice).
  • Listing consultations for sellers, lease signings and move-in walkthroughs book the same way.

Qualifies and hands off the lead

  • Buyer, seller, renter or landlord, with what they're looking for: area, budget, bedrooms, move-in date, pre-approved or not, selling a home first.
  • Sellers, landlords and pre-approved buyers text you immediately as hot leads. Everyone else lands in your Leads list with notes.
  • Texts the Information About Brokerage Services notice to every new lead, once, so it's on file.
  • Offers your lender partner to buyers who aren't pre-approved, only if they say yes.

Gets the paperwork signed

  • Showing agreement, rental application, buyer and seller intake sheets, or your own document, signed from a text link in under two minutes.
  • Signer verifies with a one-time code to their phone, consents to e-sign, draws or types their signature.
  • You get a PDF with the signature and a signing certificate: who, phone verified, every timestamp and device, tamper-proof fingerprint of the file.
  • Rental application fee paid by card to your account the moment they sign. Donna never holds money.

Rentals and the homes you manage

One number for the renter looking and the tenant who already moved in.

Many agents manage a few rentals for clients, or their own. Turn on property management and the same Donna handles both sides of that phone: the prospect asking about pets, and the tenant whose water heater quit.

For the renter

  • Availability, rent, deposit, pets, minimum lease, move-in date, from your records.
  • Books the showing and sends the showing agreement.
  • Sends the rental application by text: identity, move-in, occupants, pets, current housing and landlord, employment and income, vehicles, emergency contact. No Social Security or ID numbers over a text link; your screening vendor collects those.
  • Application fee to your account on signing; you're texted when it's in.

For the tenant

  • Recognizes the tenant and their home from the number they call from.
  • Logs a maintenance request with the problem, access instructions and permission to enter, gives them a reference number, and texts you (or your vendor).
  • Emergencies (burst pipe, gas smell, no heat in a freeze, break-in) get the safety step first, then page whoever is on call.
  • Rent questions, lease dates, renewal interest and move-out notice all get taken down properly.

Built for a licensed business

She says what's in your records. Nothing she'd have to be licensed to say.

Donna is a receptionist, not an agent. The guardrails are written into her, not left to chance.

Fair Housing, every call

  • Never characterizes a neighborhood, schools, "safety" or "what kind of people live there." Offers the same homes to every caller.
  • Answers "is it a good area?" with facts she has (distance, HOA, year built) and an offer to have you call.
  • Treats every caller the same on price, availability and showings.

No advice she can't give

  • Never quotes commission, never gives a home value or price opinion, never gives legal or lending advice. Those become a callback for you.
  • Never takes a card number, Social Security number or ID number by phone.
  • Never says when an occupied home is empty. Never reads a lockbox code aloud.

Paperwork that holds up

  • Every signature carries a certificate: phone verified by one-time code, consent to e-sign, timestamps for sent, opened, verified and signed, IP and device, a SHA-256 fingerprint of the PDF. ESIGN and UETA statement on the page.
  • Signed documents live in your account, downloadable any time. Showing agreements signed in the last six months aren't re-sent to the same person.
  • Contracts that belong in the transaction file stay in your transaction system; Donna handles the forms that come before.

Your data stays yours

  • Listings, leads, documents, call recordings and transcripts are in your account only. Nothing is shared between agents or brokerages.
  • Every call is transcribed and summarized; every text she sends is in your inbox.
  • Export listings and leads to a spreadsheet whenever you like.
