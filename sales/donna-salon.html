The problem

Every call you miss is a booking that walks to the salon down the street.

Clients call nights, weekends and in the middle of your busiest hour, which is exactly when nobody is at the desk. Most will not leave a voicemail. They just book somewhere else.

Saturday 9 PMis when people finally sit down to book. The desk closed at 6.
No-showshappen when nobody confirmed, nobody reminded, and nobody filled the hole.
Hands busyYour stylists are with clients. The phone, the texts and the DMs pile up anyway.

What salon owners told us they need

  • Someone who knows that highlights take three hours and a haircut forty-five minutes, and books the right stylist in the right gap.
  • Confirmations and reminders by text, because nobody reads email, and a way for clients to say "C" and be done.
  • Combos booked back to back ("cut and color with Mai") without a phone tag.
  • Regulars recognized: "your usual with An?"
  • Cancellations filled from a waitlist before the hole shows up in the day.
  • Allergies and color formulas where the stylist sees them, not in a text thread.

What Donna does

One front desk. The whole job.

Answers the phone, 24/7

  • Picks up in under two rings, in 25 languages, in your salon's name and the voice you pick.
  • Knows every service, how long it takes and what it costs; answers "how much is a balayage?" in one sentence.
  • Says your stylists' names right (you tell her how they sound).
  • Recognizes regulars by number: their usual service, their usual stylist, their next appointment.

Books the right chair

  • Each stylist's own hours and book. Solo? Your hours are the hours. Team? Donna offers the stylist they asked for, or whoever is open first.
  • Offers times in the window the client asked for: "after 2", "Saturday morning".
  • Combos booked back to back as one visit, one confirmation.
  • Reschedules and cancels existing bookings, found by the caller's number. Two on the same day? She asks which.
  • Syncs to Google or Microsoft calendar, and each stylist can connect their own.

Text-first, like clients expect

  • Confirmation by text: "You're booked for Haircut with An on Thursday at 4. Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule, or CANCEL."
  • Reminder the day before at 6. Replies handled on the spot: R offers open times and moves it; CANCEL frees the chair and the waitlist fills it.
  • "Running 10 late" texts the stylist. "When's my appointment?" gets an answer. Anything else lands in your Inbox to text back.
  • Donna can text the booking link, your address or hours during a call.

Keeps the book full

  • Waitlist that books the next person who fits the opening and wanted that time of day, then texts them.
  • "Time for your next color": a nudge at each service's cadence with a one-tap link to the same stylist.
  • Win-back for clients you haven't seen; thank-you and review ask after the visit.
  • Promotions to your whole list by text or email, when you choose.

Client card and intake

  • Allergies and sensitivities in red, color formulas, preferences and stylist notes on every client.
  • Donna reads the flags before booking a chemical service and asks new clients about sensitivities.
  • Consultation and consent forms sent by text before color or lash work; "Form completed" shows on the appointment.

Your own booking page and your team

  • A branded page with your logo, colors and photo, verified by a one-time code. Clients pick service, stylist and time.
  • Put the Book button on Google, Instagram, Facebook and your website in a few taps; print the QR for the mirror.
  • Every stylist gets a login, a Today view with their clients and flags, a Running-late button, and their own booking link for their bio.
