The problem

You can't answer the phone with your hands in a panel. The job goes to whoever did.

Most people who get your voicemail call the next name on the list. The ones who do leave a message want a callback you don't have time for, and then they need an invoice you'll write at 10 PM.

Missed callsare missed jobs. Nobody waits for a callback when the AC is out.

What Donna does

She's the office. You're the truck.

Answers every call

  • Picks up 24/7, in 25 languages. Switches when the caller does.
  • Knows your services, trip fee, hours, after-hours policy and service area. Only says what you set in your dashboard.
  • Asks the two or three questions your trade needs so the tech arrives ready: heat or cool, breaker tripping, is the water off, gate code, dog in the yard.
  • Recognizes repeat customers and their address.

Books the visit

  • Gets the address before offering times. Only offers openings for a tech who does that trade, covers that zip, and has travel time to get there.
  • Confirmation and day-before reminder by text.
  • Recurring routes for mowing, pool, filters and pest: set it once (weekly, every other week, monthly, quarterly, in season) and the visits land on the tech's day with the price attached.
  • Optional card on file at booking for the services you choose. Nothing is charged until the work is done.

Runs your day by text

  • OMW 20: the customer gets "Joe is on the way, about 20 minutes."
  • LATE 15, HERE, NEXT for the next stop with a map link, MOVE, CANCEL JOB, or send a photo to pin it to the job.
  • DONE: Donna lists the work, asks about add-ons, totals it, and asks: send the invoice, charge the card on file, hold it, or mark it paid?
  • Or just talk to her: "tell Maria I'm ten out", "what's next", "Dan paid cash". She asks when she needs more.
  • Or use the phone app: big buttons, Face ID, English or Spanish.

Gets you paid

  • The customer gets a text with the total and a Pay button, pays from their phone, and gets a receipt. You get a text when it lands.
  • Cash or check? Tap "They paid me." Receipt goes out, invoice closed.
  • Estimates: your tech writes every number; Donna texts the quote, nudges after three days, and books the job the moment the customer taps Approve.
  • Optional card processing fee (you pick the percent) only when they pay by card. Every invoice and payment exports to a spreadsheet.

After hours

2 AM, the pipe burst. Here's what happens.

You decide the policy once: take after-hours calls or don't, charge a flat fee or a multiplier, and whether the fee is paid before anyone rolls. Donna follows it on every call.

If you take after-hours calls

  • Donna gives the safety step first: gas smell means leave the building and call 911; water means shut the main.
  • She quotes your after-hours charge once, plainly, and asks if they want to go ahead.
  • She gets the full address and the problem, then pages the first tech on your on-call list by text.
  • No reply in 15 minutes (you set the number)? She pages the next tech, then you. Whoever texts ACK gets the job; the others are told it's covered.
  • Prepay option: the caller is texted a secure link to pay the after-hours fee first, and the tech is paged the moment it's paid. The fee is applied to the visit.

If you don't

  • Donna books the next regular opening and tells the caller when to expect you.
  • True emergencies still get the safety advice and an urgent message you see first thing.
  • Switch the policy any time from your phone. No code, no call to support.

Your money

Your Stripe. Your bank. We never touch a dollar.

Most software in this market holds your payments, charges you to get them faster, and leaves you alone when a customer disputes a charge. Donna doesn't take payments at all. Your customers pay you.

How it works

  • Tap Connect Stripe in Donna once. Have a Stripe account? Sign in. Don't? The same button creates one in about five minutes.
  • Every Pay button, every card on file, every charge runs through your Stripe account. The money goes to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule.
  • Stripe's standard card fee applies (2.9% + 30¢). Donna adds nothing. Want the customer to cover it? Turn on the card processing fee and pick the percent; cash and check never pay it.
  • Refunds, disputes and payouts: your Stripe dashboard, same as a card reader.

Don't want card payments?

  • Skip the Stripe step. Donna still sends the invoice by text and you tap "paid" when they hand you the check. The customer still gets a receipt.
  • Turn it on later with one tap. Nothing else changes.
  • Your customer list, your jobs, your estimates and your invoices are yours. Export any time.

Built for trust

  • Donna never prices a job. She quotes the trip fee and after-hours charge you set, and nothing else. Estimates are written line by line by your tech; over a limit you choose, you approve them before the customer sees them.
  • Nothing goes to the customer until you say so. You see the lines and the total before you tap Send or Charge.
  • Every call is transcribed and summarized; every text she sends is in your Inbox. Photos your techs text in are on the job.
  • Your customers and your settings stay in your account. Nothing is shared between businesses.
