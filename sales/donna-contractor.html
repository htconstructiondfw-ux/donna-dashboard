What Donna does

She's the office. You're the truck.

Answers every call

  • Picks up 24/7, in 25 languages. Switches when the caller does.
  • Knows your services, prices, trip fee, service area and hours. Only says what you approved.
  • Asks the right two or three questions for your trade so the tech arrives ready: heat or cool, breaker tripping, is the water shut off, gate code, dog in the yard.
  • Recognizes repeat customers and their address.

Books the visit

  • Service calls, estimates and the work itself, each with its own length and fee.
  • Address required on every job. Confirmation and day-before reminder by text, from your number.
  • Recurring routes for mowing, pool and lawn: book the season once.
  • Customers can book from the link on your truck, card or Facebook page.

Runs your day by text

  • Text OMW 20 and the customer gets "Joe is on the way, about 20 minutes."
  • Text LATE 15, HERE, NEXT for the next stop with a map link, or send a photo to pin it to the job.
  • Text DONE. Donna lists the work, asks about add-ons, totals it, and asks: send the invoice now, hold it, or mark it paid?
  • Or use the phone app: four big buttons, no training.

Gets you paid

  • The customer gets a text with the total and a Pay button. They pay from their phone. You get a text when it lands.
  • Cash or check? Tap "They paid me." Invoice closed.
  • Every invoice and payment exports to a spreadsheet or QuickBooks.
  • Emergencies page the on-call tech by text and escalate if nobody answers.

Your money

Your Stripe. Your bank. We never touch a dollar.

Most software in this market holds your payments, charges you to get them faster, and leaves you alone when a customer disputes a charge. Donna doesn't take payments at all. Your customers pay you.

How it works

  • Tap Connect Stripe in Donna once. Have a Stripe account already? Sign in. Don't? The same button creates one in about five minutes.
  • From then on, every Pay button on your invoices runs through your Stripe account. The money goes to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule.
  • Stripe's standard card fee applies (2.9% + 30¢). Donna adds nothing. No instant-payout surcharge, no per-invoice fee.
  • Refunds, disputes and payouts: your Stripe dashboard, same as a card reader.

Don't want card payments?

  • Skip the Stripe step. Donna still sends the invoice by text and you tap "paid" when they hand you the check.
  • Turn it on later with one tap. Nothing else changes.
  • Either way, your customer list, your jobs and your invoices are yours. Export any time.

Built for trust

  • Donna never quotes a price that isn't on your list. Anything else: "the tech will give you a firm price before any work starts."
  • Nothing goes to the customer until you say so. You see the lines and the total before you tap Send.
  • Safety first on emergencies: gas smell means "leave the building and call 911" before anything else.
  • Every call is transcribed and summarized; every text she sends is in your Inbox.
  • Your customers and your knowledge stay in your account. Nothing is shared between businesses.
