The problem

Every missed call is a customer who called the next name on the list.

You are on a ladder, in a car, with a client, or finally asleep. The phone rings anyway. Most people will not leave a voicemail, and most will not call back.

Most callershang up on voicemail and try someone else. The job goes to whoever answered.
Same 5 questionsover and over: how much, how long, do you come to me, when can you do it, how do I pay.
Zero hoursin the day left for callbacks, confirmations and reminders. So no-shows happen.

What owners told us they want

  • Someone who answers like they work there: knows the services, the prices, the hours, the area you cover.
  • Real bookings on a real calendar, not "someone will call you back".
  • A text confirmation the customer can actually find, and a reminder the day before so they show up.
  • A clear message when it is something only you can answer, with the number to call back.
  • No software to learn. No one on your payroll. Nothing to babysit.

What Donna does

A front desk that works nights, weekends and holidays.

Answers every call

  • Picks up in under two rings, 24/7, in 25 languages. Switches when the caller does.
  • Introduces herself with your business name, in the voice you pick.
  • Recognizes returning customers by their number and remembers what they usually book.
  • Only says what you approved. If she doesn't know, she takes a message instead of guessing.

Answers the questions

  • Prices, how long things take, your hours, your service area, how to pay, cancellation policy, what to have ready.
  • Paste your website or type a few answers once. Donna turns them into replies you approve before she uses them.
  • Can text the caller your address with a map link, your hours, or a price so they have it in writing.

Books real appointments

  • Knows each service and how long it takes, so a 3-hour job never gets squeezed into a 30-minute gap.
  • Offers only open times from your hours and your calendar, in the window the caller asked for ("Saturday morning").
  • Takes the name and, for work at the customer's place, the address, and reads it back.
  • Reschedules and cancels existing bookings too, found by the caller's number.
  • Syncs to Google or Microsoft calendar, and each team member can connect their own.

Texts like a good assistant

  • Confirmation by text the moment it's booked, with the address.
  • Reminder the day before: "Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule, or CANCEL". Donna handles those replies herself.
  • "Running 10 minutes late" from a customer reaches you by text.
  • Anything else they text lands in your Inbox, where you can text back with one tap.

Takes a proper message

  • Who, what, best number, in the caller's own words. Emailed to you and kept in the dashboard.
  • Urgent calls are flagged so you see them first.
  • Transfers live to your cell during the hours you choose; takes the message when you can't pick up.

Lets customers book themselves

  • A clean booking page with your logo and colors, verified by a one-time code so no one books under someone else's name.
  • Put the Book button on Google, Instagram, Facebook and your website in a few taps; print the QR code for the counter.
  • Donna can text the link to anyone who would rather book later.

Keeping customers

Donna doesn't just take the call. She brings people back.

Reminders that cut no-shows

The day before, by text and email, with the service, the time and the place. Customers confirm with one letter. If they need to move, Donna offers the open times and moves it on the spot.

Thank-you and review asks

A few hours after the visit, a short text thanking them with your Google review link. One ask per customer, never to someone who cancelled.

"Time for your next one"

Win-back

1

Open days and times. Whether customers come to you or you go to them.

3
What people can book.

Name, how long, price. "Full detail, 3 hours, $220." That's it.

4

Your cell for transfers during the day, your email for messages.
