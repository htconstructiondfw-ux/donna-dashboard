How Donna works

She finds the property first, then follows that property's playbook.

Every property in your portfolio is tagged as a residential rental, a commercial or flex building, or a community association. Donna recognizes the caller, asks which property if she has to, and runs the right rules for the rest of the call.

Residential rentals

  • Repairs inside the unit are yours: she gathers the problem, unit, permission to enter and access notes, then opens the work order.
  • Suggests the quick fix first (breaker, GFCI, disposal reset) and notes it if it worked.
  • Rent: when it's due and where to pay. Never takes card numbers.
  • Renters insurance: texts the tenant a secure upload link for their policy.
  • Showings and inspections booked at the unit, address on the confirmation.

Commercial and flex

  • Base-building problems (roof, elevator, lobby, mains) are yours; in-suite items follow the lease, and Donna reads back what applies.
  • After-hours heating and cooling requests with your lead time and "billable per lease" note built in.
  • Access cards, keys, visitor pre-registration, after-hours entry.
  • Knows who the authorized contacts are at each tenant company and says when sign-off is needed.
  • Certificates of insurance from tenants and vendors, with an upload link.

Communities (HOA and condo)

  • Common areas, gates, pools and the building shell are the association's; in-home problems get the right guidance, not a work order.
  • ARC requests taken in full, with the review clock and "don't start until approved" explained, and an upload link for drawings.
  • Violation reports taken neutrally; owners asking about their own notice hear the cure and hearing process.
  • Resale certificates and lender questionnaires logged with the statutory due date.
  • Gate codes released only to verified residents, read digit by digit. Amenity reservations with fee, deposit and rules.
  • Board and annual meetings: dates, agenda, RSVPs and proxies, with the running quorum count.

Wearing two hats

Manage rentals inside a community you also manage? Donna routes the renter's hot-water call to the managed home and the owner's fence request to the association, on the same phone number, without asking you to sort it out.
